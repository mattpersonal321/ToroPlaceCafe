The building

Still has the gas pumps out front.

Toro Place Cafe is a converted gas station from the 1920s and 30s — once the residence of the original owners, now a diner with the vintage pumps still standing sentry by the door. It's the kind of place where the front screen door opens like you're walking into someone's home, because for a while, you were.

Sitting right on Highway 68 between Monterey and Salinas, it's become the community's unofficial front porch — regulars on a first-name basis, a toy drive every year, and a patio that welcomes your dog along with your appetite.
